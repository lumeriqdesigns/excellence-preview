Free JAMB & WAEC CBT practice for Nigerian students

MeritScholars is an online CBT practice platform built for secondary school students preparing for JAMB UTME and WAEC (WASSCE). Take timed mock exams, browse 76,000+ practice questions, track weak topics with progress analytics, compete on the leaderboard, and ask the Merit AI Tutor for explanations.

Start free in your browser, install the app on your phone, and practise anywhere — including offline after the question bank has loaded. Select Premium to unlock the full bank for free in this preview.

Frequently asked questions

What is MeritScholars CBT?

MeritScholars CBT is a free online practice platform for Nigerian secondary school students preparing for JAMB UTME and WAEC (WASSCE). It offers timed computer-based tests, a bank of 76,000+ practice questions, progress analytics, a leaderboard, and an AI tutor.

Is MeritScholars free for JAMB and WAEC practice?

Yes. You can start free with limited questions per test. Premium preview access is free and unlocks the full question bank. No payment or subscription is required.

How do I practise JAMB CBT online?

Open MeritScholars, choose JAMB Practice or the Custom CBT Simulator, select up to four subjects, set the number of questions, and start a timed mock exam that follows UTME-style CBT format.

Does MeritScholars have WAEC past questions?

Yes. The question bank includes WAEC (WASSCE) objective-style practice across major subjects. Filter by exam type, subject, and topic, then review scores and weak areas in Progress Analytics.

Which subjects can I practise?

Use of English, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Government, Economics, Literature-in-English, Commerce, Financial Accounting, Agricultural Science, CRK, and IRS.

What is the Merit AI Tutor?

The CBT question bank works without an AI subscription. Merit AI is disabled in this public preview.

Can I use MeritScholars offline?

MeritScholars is a Progressive Web App (PWA). After you open the site and install it to your home screen, much of the practice experience can work offline once question data has been loaded.
